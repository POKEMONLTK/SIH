2. 155 mm Artillery Aerodynamics & Despun Canard Mechanics

Standard 155 mm projectiles (M107 / M795, 43.5 kg) are fired from 52-caliber howitzers (e.g., ATAGS, K9 Vajra) at muzzle velocities between 800 and 830 m/s. The barrel's 1:20 twist rifling imparts an intense axial spin rate:

p0 = 2π • V0η • d ≈ 2π • 827 m/s20 • 0.155 m ≈ 1,676 rad/s  •  267 rev/s (16,000 RPM)

2.1 NATO STANAG 4355 Modified Point Mass (MPM) Equations

The projectile's center-of-mass acceleration is governed by the vector sum of gravity, Earth rotation (Coriolis acceleration), aerodynamic drag, aerodynamic lift, and the Magnus cross-force:

a = g − 2(Ω × v) − ρ S CD(M) v2 m v + ρ S CL(M) v22 m αt + ρ S d Cmag(M) p2 m (v × δ)

Transonic Drag Rise Curve (CD vs. Mach): Drag remains low at subsonic speeds (CD ≈ 0.22 for M < 0.8), spikes sharply through the transonic barrier to peak at CD ≈ 0.44 at Mach 1.05, and gradually decays exponentially toward CD ≈ 0.28 at Mach 2.4.

4. Guidance, Navigation & Control (GNC) Architecture

Artillery precision guidance differs fundamentally from guided missile flight: shells carry no rocket motor and must preserve forward kinetic energy. Excessive steering increases drag and drastically reduces range.

4.1 Predictive Impact Point (PIP) & Zero-Effort-Miss (ZEM) Guidance

Rather than continuously applying lateral acceleration, the onboard computer performs high-speed numerical trajectory projection:

  1. At each guidance cycle (10 Hz), project the unguided ballistic arc from the current state (x̂k, v̂k) to ground altitude (z = 0), yielding predicted impact coordinates PPIP = [Xpred, Ypred]T.
  2. Calculate the Zero-Effort-Miss (ZEM) vector relative to the intended target Ptarget:
    eZEM = Ptarget − PPIP = [ΔR, ΔD]T  •  (Range Error, Deflection Error)
  3. Correction Strategy:
    • If ΔR < 0 (overshoot): Rotate canards to neutral pitch and pulse the alternator brake to maximize aerodynamic drag.
    • If ΔR > 0 (undershoot): Minimize all drag; maintain maximum ballistic glide velocity.
    • If |ΔD| > 5 m: Orient the despun nose roll angle φcmd = arctan2(−ΔD, ΔR) to steer the projectile back into the target corridor.

5. Cyber-Resilience Engine & Anti-Spoofing Architecture

Modern electronic warfare units deploy GPS spoofers that broadcast synthetically delayed or coordinate-offset pseudoranges to pull precision munitions away from targets. The proposed digital twin incorporates a multi-tier defense engine.

5.1 Error-State Extended Kalman Filter (ESKF) Formulation

The navigation filter estimates the 15-state error vector: position errors δp, velocity errors δv, attitude error angles δθ, accelerometer bias ba, and gyroscope bias bg. High-rate IMU strapdown dead-reckoning operates at 100 Hz, while GNSS updates arrive at 10 Hz.

Adversarial Threat: Coordinated GPS Spoofing Attack
An adversary injects a smooth +200 m coordinate bias to steer the shell into an empty hillside. If a standard Kalman filter blindly incorporates this observation, the shell will maneuver aggressively into collateral zones.

5.2 High-Speed Chi-Square (χ2) Innovation Residual Gate

Before any GNSS measurement is allowed to update the state covariance matrix, it must pass a Receiver Autonomous Integrity Monitoring (RAIM) hypothesis test:

yk = zGNSS − Hk x̂k−  •  (Innovation Residual)
Sk = Hk Pk− HkT + RGNSS  •  (Innovation Covariance)
γk = ykT Sk−1 yk  •  (Normalized Innovation Squared - NIS)

Under nominal conditions, γk follows a Chi-square distribution χ2(m) with m = 3 degrees of freedom. At a significance level of α = 0.01, the critical rejection threshold is:

γk > χ20.01, 3 = 11.34 ⇒ REJECT MEASUREMENT & FLAG SPOOFING ATTACK

5.3 Dynamic 4-Factor Navigation Confidence Score

The onboard system maintains a real-time health and confidence metric Ct ∈ [0%, 100%] evaluated as:

Ct = w1 (1 − γkγmax) + w2 (Nsats12) + w3 (1 − ScoreAI) + w4 (11 + Tr(Ppos))

When an attack occurs, γk spikes to >35.0, satellite lock flags fail consistency checks, and the confidence metric plunges to < 10% within 200 ms. This triggers the MIL-STD-1316 state machine to transition immediately into INHIBITED (SAFE DUD).
